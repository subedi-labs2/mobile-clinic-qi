# 00 — Project Charter

## Project title
Standardizing Social Needs Screening and Referral at a Mobile Clinic Serving People Experiencing Homelessness (Chantilly, VA)

## Problem statement
The mobile clinic serves about 30 adults experiencing homelessness in western Fairfax County. Staff do not use a standard way to ask about social needs. Whether a patient is asked about food, transportation, safety, or documents depends on the clinician. Positive answers are not documented in a consistent place, and referral follow-up is not tracked. As a result, needs go unidentified, referrals are inconsistent, and the clinic cannot tell whether patients are connected to services.

## Background
- Unmet health-related social needs (HRSNs) are associated with worse health outcomes and higher acute-care use. For people experiencing homelessness, several HRSNs often occur together.
- Standardized instruments exist, such as CMS AHC-HRSN and NACHC PRAPARE. Their value depends on a reliable workflow and a referral pathway the clinic can actually deliver (see [01](01-evidence-review.md)).

## SMART aim (global)
> By the end of the 12-week intervention period, **≥ 80 %** of adult patient visits will have an up-to-date (completed within 90 days) standardized social needs screen (baseline estimated < 30 %), and **≥ 90 %** of patients with a positive screen will be offered a referral for every positive domain at the same visit.

Secondary aim: **≥ 50 %** of accepted referrals will have a documented follow-up outcome within 30 days.

Targets are provisional. Replace the baseline estimate after the baseline chart audit ([07](07-measurement-plan.md)).

## Measures (summary — see [07](07-measurement-plan.md))
- **Process:** % visits with up-to-date screen; % due screens completed; % positive screens offered referral.
- **Outcome:** % accepted referrals with confirmed connection within 30 days.
- **Balancing:** added visit time; patient decline rate; staff-reported burden.

## Risks and mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Screening for needs the clinic cannot address erodes trust | Verified resource directory before go-live; ask whether the patient wants help |
| Safety disclosures in a non-private mobile space | Ask safety items only when privacy is possible; script for deferral |
| Patients hard to reach for follow-up | Collect best contact method; follow up at the next clinic visit |
| Small sample (n≈30) limits statistical inference | Use run charts and run rules, not hypothesis tests |
| Staff turnover/volunteers | Brief job aid, champion model, training in onboarding |
