# 06 — Implementation Plan

Framework: **Model for Improvement** (Langley et al.) with sequential PDSA cycles, reported with **SQUIRE 2.0**.

## Phases (default 16 weeks; timeline not yet decided)

| Phase | Weeks | Key activities | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Prepare** | −4 to 0 | Sponsor approval; QI/IRB determination; verify resources; patient-advisor review; train staff; print materials | Approvals done; 100 % screeners pass competency |
| **1. Baseline** | 1–4 | Measure current practice: chart audit of all adult visits (was *any* social need asked and documented? referral?) | ≥ 4 weekly data points |
| **2. PDSA 1** | 5–8 | Champion screens every patient who is due, at intake (paper) | Screening ≥ 60 %; workflow time known |
| **3. PDSA 2** | 9–12 | Spread to all trained staff; add desire-for-help and referral-offer step | Screening ≥ 80 %; referral offer ≥ 90 % |
| **4. PDSA 3** | 13–16 | Add 14-/30-day follow-up and closed-loop tracking | Follow-up documented ≥ 50 % |
| **5. Sustain** | 17+ | Add to onboarding; monthly audit; hand off to clinic owner | Sustained ≥ 80 % for 3 months |

## PDSA cycle plans

### PDSA 1: Champion-led screening at intake
- **Plan:** A single trained champion screens every adult who is due, at intake with the paper form. *Prediction:* screening rises from about 25 % to at least 60 %, with fewer than 8 minutes added per visit.
- **Do:** Run for 4 weeks. Log visit time, declines, and barriers (no private space, interpreter needed).
- **Study:** Review the weekly run chart, decline rate, and time per screen. Debrief the champion.
- **Act:** Adopt, adapt (for example, move screening to the waiting period), or abandon.

### PDSA 2: Spread + referral offer
- **Plan:** All trained staff screen. Each positive domain gets the desire-for-help question and a referral offer. *Prediction:* screening ≥ 80 % and referral offer ≥ 90 %.
- **Study:** Compare screening rate by screener role (fidelity) and the referral funnel.

### PDSA 3: Closed-loop follow-up
- **Plan:** The referral owner follows up at 14 ± 7 days and again by 30 days. *Prediction:* ≥ 50 % of accepted referrals have a documented outcome.
- **Study:** Track the unable-to-reach rate and which contact methods work.

## Stakeholder and communication plan
| Who | What | When |
|---|---|---|
| Clinic staff | Run chart on the wall/van + huddle | Weekly |
| Clinic sponsor | 1-page update | Every 4 weeks |
| Faculty advisor | Progress + data | Per program schedule |
| Community partners (WFCM, shelters, CSP) | Heads-up on expected referral volume; feedback | Before go-live + monthly |
| Patients | Resource card; "we ask everyone" signage | Ongoing |

## Barriers and mitigations
| Barrier | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Limited private space in the van | Use an outdoor/curtained area for safety items, or defer and record "deferred" |
| Interpreters | Phone interpreter line; translated forms |
| Volunteer/staff turnover | Job aid; champion; onboarding module |
| Resources at capacity | Re-verify quarterly; track "not connected" reasons |
| Patients without phones | Collect a shelter/case-manager contact; follow up at the next stop; Lifeline referral |

## Sustainability
- Add the screening to the standard visit template.
- Assign an owner for monthly audit (10 random charts).
- Re-verify the resource directory quarterly.
